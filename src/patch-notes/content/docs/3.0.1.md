# WarCraft III: Reforged - Patch 3.0.1

The patch was initially released on October 8th and later reverted.

## **Audio**

DE graphics will now use classic sounds.

## **Campaign**

### **The Last Days of Lordaeron**

- Increased the health and damage of most bosses in this campaign.
- Garek’s Sweeping Strike damage decreased from 100/175/250 to 100/150/200
- Ilastar’s Surge of Light no longer procs while attacking destructibles.
- Ilastar’s Sacred Rebuke Talent now reduces the correct amount of Magic Resist on enemies.
- Players are no longer able to return to the throne room after the cutscene with Uther.
- Ilastar’s Mind Control no longer allows building from Acolytes.
- Units can no longer run out map bounds on some maps.

### **Forsaken Kingdom**

- Overall campaign difficulty has been increased
  - In general, enemies deal increased damage and are more durable.
- Forsaken Kingdom campaign Heroes have had their base and per level attributes adjusted.
- Rebalanced several consumable items across the campaign.
- Fixed an issue where consumable items were not properly stacking in Act 2\.
- Rebalanced many Equipment items.
- Added a Tome of Retraining to several vendors.
  - Tomes of Retraining will also reset talent points.
- Act 1: Silverpine Sprint \- Added more fences and wider run-off areas. Collisions are now less likely to flip over the car.
- Act 1: Silverpine Sprint \- Miko Screwmaker and the other drivers all had to remove their illegal engine modifications and are now several seconds slower.
- Act 1: Silverpine Sprint \- Spacebar has been added as a brake key.
- Garek’s Relentless Cleave damage decreased from 100/175/250/325/400 to 100/150/200/250/300.
- Garek’s Undying Defiance movement penalty reduced from 40%/40%/40%/40%/40% to 40%/35%/30%/25%/20%.
- Deathseeker Arrows now stack with other attack effects, including:
  - Critical Strikes
  - Poison-tipped Arrows talent
  - Orbs
- Anya’s Umbral Rupture talent no longer damages allies
- Items Mark of the Phoenix, Deepsea Bag, Heart of the Firebender, Bindings of Helya and Essencium will now spawn projectiles as intended

## **Neutral**

- Forsaken Paladin hotkey changed from F to Y to prevent conflicts from heroes of other races.
- Forsaken Paladin Righteous Fury attack rate slow increased from 30%/40%/50% to 40%/40%/40%.
- Forsaken Paladin Righteous Fury movement speed slow increased from 20%/30%/40% to 40%/40%/40%.
- Forsaken Paladin Righteous Fury mana cost reduced from 100/100/100 to 90/90/90
- Forsaken Paladin Righteous Fury damage reduced from 100/150/200 to 90/135/180
- Forsaken Paladin Consecration no longer affects air and mechanical units.
- Forsaken Paladin Consecration duration increased from 5 to 10\.
- Forsaken Paladin Consecration cooldown increased from 8 to 10\.
- Forsaken Paladin Consecration healing reduced from 25/35/50 to 15/25/35.
- Forsaken Paladin Consecration damage reduced from 20/30/40 15/20/25.
- Forsaken Paladin Sacred Aura Magic Resistance reduced from 15%/25%/35% to 15%/20%/25%.
- Forsaken Paladin Cleansing Fire no longer dispells nondispellable spells such as Windwalk and Doom.
- Forsaken Paladin Cleansing Fire damage buff now properly stacks with other damage buffs.
